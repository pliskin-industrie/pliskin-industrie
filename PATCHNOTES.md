# Pliskin Industrie 0.8.2 – Patch notes

**Fitting for all ships.** Until now the fitting only covered the Gladius, Golem and Polaris. Now 213 ships are in, the maths has been rebuilt and the parts have their own images too.

## New
- **Fitting for all ships:** weapons, missiles, turrets, components and paints for 213 ships. The images (front, top, side) are rendered by us from the game files, in three stages (A, B, C).
- **“Bild folgt” (image coming):** where an image is still missing, a clean placeholder in the matching design replaces the broken image. The fitting still works fully.
- **New fitting layout:** three views side by side, boxes left and right of the ship, tidy lines.
- **Hangar picker:** ships as cards with a picture, filterable by manufacturer and role.
- **Part comparison, drafts and sharing:** compare two parts directly, up to 4 drafts per ship, share a loadout as text and load it again.
- **Images for parts:** weapons, missiles, components and other parts now have their own images, also rendered from the game files.
- **More values in the fitting:** header tiles with the key values, power split into SCM and NAV, cooling, signatures, quantum range and travel time, sustained weapon damage and boost recharge.
- **Desktop view on phones:** “Desktop site” in the phone browser shows the full interface. A button switches back to the phone view.

## Star Citizen 4.10.3
- **Game data updated to 4.10.3** (build 13165946): recipes, values, ships, missions, catalog and knowledge.
- **New ships in the fitting, with images:** Anvil Liberator, Drake Marauder, Gatac Hyun and RSI Galaxy.
- **16 new blueprints**, e.g. M5A Cannon Raider Camo (S1–S3), Skarnov “Raider Camo” rifle and magazine, Palatino/Corbel Raider Camo, Chiron/Geist Epoque II, Tailwind Flight Suit Raider Camo with helmet, Quirinus backpack – all in the catalog with images and mission pools.
- **Changed ships and parts** follow the patch, e.g. Vanguard weapons, radar size of Starlancer and Perseus, Centaurus hydrogen tank, Vanduul weapons. Ship images were re-rendered where the patch changed something.
- **StarStrings marks** now also work for missions with several blueprint pools (“Pool 1”, “Pool 2” …) and lists like “Potential Blueprints (Neutral only)”. Checked against MrKraken's StarStrings for 4.10.3.
- The patch number and build are shown at the bottom of the program and in the fitting.

## Improved
- **Cargo grid (storage and tour):** crates are drawn in the right order and lying flat. Crate images and material symbols load reliably. If not all crates fit, another freight elevator opens instead of crates disappearing.
- **Fitting maths corrected:** power, pips, cooling, shields, signatures, quantum drive and weapon values are now calculated from the game files and checked against Erkul. Crafted power plants add whole pips. Salvage and tractor beams, EMP and quantum dampeners count as consumers too.
- **Shield pips:** all shields share one pip bar. Saved loadouts are converted automatically.
- **Fitting images:** The front view is never narrower than the ship and keeps the real image shape (e.g. Golem). The ship images have been reworked: modular weapons sit on the weapon, external attachments are visible, the image frames fit. All 213 ships have their images, now including the Perseus.
- **Parts without a name in the game** (e.g. salvage arms, the Perseus torpedo racks) are no longer missing.
- **English** now also for capture, the craft window and the storage view, plus empty lists, refineries and the gold hint.
- **Offline page** now shows the icon in the browser tab (was a 404 before).
- **Help:** two new notes on capture (server mode for the phone trigger, same rights as Star Citizen for the in-game hotkey).

## Known
- A few images may still be missing; those show “Bild folgt”.
- Values come without guarantee. Not measured in the game, for example: how shield pips spread across several shields. The main purpose stays the crafting plan.
- With all the images the program has become noticeably larger.
- A few missile racks on the new ships are not shown on the ship images yet.
- **Ship modules missing:** Swappable modules (e.g. RSI Galaxy cargo module, RSI Apollo modules) cannot be selected in the fitting yet, so their effects (more cargo grid, torpedo bay etc.) are missing. Coming in the next version.
- **Components only selectable via the menu:** Slots can only be picked and changed with the arrow buttons in the menu. In future they will be clickable directly in the ship view. Coming in the next version.
- Data: Star Citizen 4.10.3.
